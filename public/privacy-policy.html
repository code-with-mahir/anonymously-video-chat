Privacy Policy for Talkoye

Last Updated: June 20, 2026

At Talkoye, accessible from talkoye.com or talkoye.onrender.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Talkoye and how we use it.

1. Anonymous Chat & Video Streams

Talkoye is an anonymous peer-to-peer video chat platform. We do not require users to create an account, register, or provide any personal details like name, email, or phone number to use the basic chat features. Your video and audio streams are transmitted directly to the peer you are connected with and are not recorded or stored on our servers.

2. Log Files & Cookies

Talkoye follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Ads

Google is one of the third-party vendors on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to talkoye.com and other sites on the internet. We use Google AdSense to serve programmatic ads. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on user visits. You may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy.

4. User Generated Content

Since the platform allows live text and video communication, users are solely responsible for the content they broadcast. We do not monitor individual private sessions actively, but users must abide by our platform safety terms. No data from chat history is archived post-session disconnect.

5. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
